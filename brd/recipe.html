How to make bread 🍞


ingredients:

  • flour.
  • water.
  • yeast.


steps:

  • first, get a bowl and put flour and water in it.
  • mix
  • knead the dough, then add yeast
  • place in a bowl, cover with a cloth and wait overnight.
  • take the kneaded dough out of the bowl
  • place it in the oven and bake at 122°C (251.6°F)
