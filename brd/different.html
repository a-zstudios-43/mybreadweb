you may already know that bread is universal, however, do you know the names?

naan is a type of bread from india that is flat, it is typically eaten with butter chicken and curries. It is simillar to another type of bread: Arepa, another flatbread, but instead, arepa is stuffed with savoury fillings. Another type of bread is Shokupan (食パン) from japan, it relies heavily more on butter and eggs, it has a milky taste, it is: "the milk bread"
